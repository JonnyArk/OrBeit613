## 2025-02-14 - Convert sensitive Firebase Functions from onRequest to onCall
**Vulnerability:** The AI generation endpoints (`generateAsset`, `distillContext`) and credit tracking (`creditUsage`) were implemented as `onRequest` functions and did not manually verify the caller's Firebase Auth token. This allowed any unauthenticated user to invoke the AI logic and burn backend AI credits (Google AI Ultra API).
**Learning:** `onRequest` functions are public HTTP endpoints that do not automatically verify Firebase Auth. While the Flutter app uses `httpsCallable` to communicate with these endpoints, it actually failed behind the scenes when calling `onRequest` endpoints due to a mismatch in payload wrapping (`data` property).
**Prevention:** Always use `onCall` for Firebase Cloud Functions that are meant to be exclusively called by the frontend app (`httpsCallable`), as `onCall` automatically decodes and verifies Firebase Auth tokens and injects them into `request.auth`.
