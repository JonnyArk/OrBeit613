## 2025-01-30 - Lazy Loading Heterogeneous Lists in Flutter
**Learning:** Passing pre-instantiated widgets into `ListView.builder` or using `ListView` directly negates lazy-loading performance benefits. To optimize heterogeneous lists while maintaining strong typing and preserving local variables, construct an array of closures (`List<Widget Function()>`) and evaluate them dynamically inside the `itemBuilder` callback. Also, index-based animations must be capped, otherwise lazily loaded items will take massive delays to fade in upon scroll.
**Action:** Always wrap list items in closures before passing them into a `ListView.builder` when the list contains dynamic or heterogeneous content to preserve lazy rendering, and ensure index-based delay logic doesn't break lazy load scrollability.
