## 2026-09-03 - Optimizing Heterogeneous Lists with ListView.builder
**Learning:** Passing pre-instantiated widgets into `ListView` or using a list of closures inside `ListView.builder` causes O(N) upfront allocations and negates lazy loading benefits for large heterogeneous lists. Staggered animations with unbounded delays (e.g., `i * 40`) cause lazily loaded items further down the list to stay invisible for seconds upon scrolling.
**Action:** When handling varied items (headers, active tasks, completed tasks), map the data models into a flat list of Dart 3 records (e.g., `({String type, dynamic data, int index})`) and dynamically evaluate them inside the `itemBuilder` callback. Always cap the index multiplier for initial entry animations (e.g., `(index > 10 ? 10 : index) * 40`) to preserve smooth scrolling.
