## YYYY-MM-DD - Optimizing heterogeneous lists in Flutter with ListView.builder and closures
**Learning:** Passing pre-instantiated widgets into an eagerly loaded ListView negates its performance benefits and causes lag with long lists. Refactoring it to `ListView.builder` by evaluating an array of closures (`List<Widget Function()>`) allows lazy loading while maintaining strong typing, preserving local variables, and preventing scroll jank caused by index-based delayed animations.
**Action:** When building heterogeneous lists in Flutter, use an array of strongly-typed closures (`List<Widget Function()>`) with `ListView.builder`, and evaluate them dynamically inside the `itemBuilder` callback. Remember to remove or adjust staggered entrance animations to prevent delayed visuals as items scroll into view.
